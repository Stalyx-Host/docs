## Who we are

Stalyx is a hosting provider built around one goal: making it simple and affordable to get your Discord bot online and keep it running reliably.

We started with a focus on **Discord bot hosting**, giving developers and community owners an easy way to deploy, manage, and scale their bots without dealing with complicated server setup or unreliable uptime.
