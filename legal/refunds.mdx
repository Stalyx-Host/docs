## 1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains when Stalyx ("we", "us", "our") issues refunds for paid services, how to request one, and the conditions that apply. By purchasing any Stalyx service, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds may be granted at our discretion when a service has not been delivered as described, when a technical issue on our end prevents use of the service, or when required by applicable consumer protection law. Refund requests must be submitted within a reasonable window from the original purchase date.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Service Failure" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Prolonged outages or infrastructure issues on our side that prevent normal service usage.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Billing Errors" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Duplicate charges, incorrect amounts, or accidental purchases reported promptly.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

Certain purchases are not eligible for refunds, including but not limited to:

- Services already used or partially consumed during the billing period
- Add-ons, upgrades, or one-time fees after activation
- Accounts terminated due to violation of our Terms of Service
- Domain registrations, licenses, or third-party costs passed through to you
- Purchases outside the eligible refund window

## 4. Refund Timeframe

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5 to 10 business days from the date of approval. The time it takes for the funds to appear in your account depends on your payment provider and may take additional time to reflect on your statement.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Processing" icon="clock">
    5 to 10 business days on our side after a refund is approved.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bank Settlement" icon="building-columns">
    Additional time may be required by your card issuer or payment provider.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## 5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our billing team with your account email, order or invoice number, and a brief description of the reason for the request. We may ask for additional information to verify the request and determine eligibility.

- Include your account email and order or invoice number
- Explain why you are requesting the refund
- Provide any supporting details such as error messages or screenshots
- Submit the request within the eligible refund window

## 6. Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us before opening a chargeback with your bank or card provider. Chargebacks filed without first attempting to resolve the issue with our team may result in account suspension until the dispute is closed. We reserve the right to contest chargebacks we believe are unwarranted.

## 7. Cancellations

You may cancel a subscription at any time from your billing panel. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically issue a refund for the current billing period. Your service will remain active until the end of the paid period unless you request early termination.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cancel Anytime" icon="circle-xmark">
    Stop future renewals from your billing panel without contacting support.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Access Until Period End" icon="calendar-check">
    Your service stays active for the remainder of the current billing cycle.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## 8. Partial Refunds

In certain cases, we may offer a partial refund based on the portion of the service that was not used, the nature of the issue reported, or as a goodwill gesture. Partial refunds are granted at our discretion and are not guaranteed.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email or through a notice on our website. Your continued use of Stalyx after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
